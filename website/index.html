The problem

Farmers grow the food. Middlemen take the margin.

Small farmers struggle to reach buyers they can trust, and buyers can't see the harvest before they pay. HarvestHub removes each of these barriers.

01

Hidden demand

Farmers don't know who wants what, or at what price.

→ Live market prices and buyer "wanted" requests
02

Trust gap

Distant buyers can't check the quality of a harvest.

→ Geotagged photos and video, graded by officers
03

Costly transport

Finding and pricing a vehicle is slow, and produce spoils.

→ Right-sized vehicle, fair fee, nearest driver
04

Payment risk

No safe way to pay a stranger in advance.

→ Escrow released only when both sides confirm
Features

Everything a harvest needs to reach the market

One connected system covers listing, verification, deals, payment, transport and delivery.

GPS-verified listings

Farmers capture photos and a short video in the app, at the farm. Location and time are locked into every file.

Officer quality grading

Agricultural officers review each harvest on the desktop portal and grade it A, B or C before buyers can see it.

Negotiate in chat

Buyer and farmer agree quantity, price and pickup date with counter-offers. The deal is locked when both confirm.

Payment-first escrow

The buyer pays a 20% advance into escrow before any vehicle moves. The farmer receives it when the goods leave the farm, and the rest is released automatically after delivery is confirmed by both driver and buyer.

Farmer fee0%
Buyer service fee3%
Advance held in escrow20%
Refund if rejected before loadingFull

Smart dispatch

The smallest vehicle that fits the load is chosen, the fee is calculated by distance and weight, and the nearest free driver gets the job.

Pre-loading approval

The driver photographs the harvest at the farm. The buyer approves it remotely before loading — no surprises on arrival.

Live tracking

Farmer and buyer follow the vehicle on a live map, with the road route, arrival time and distance left.

Trust scores

Ratings and completed deliveries build a public trust score for every farmer, buyer and driver.

How it works

From harvest to payment in eight clear steps

Every order follows the same safe path. Each party always knows the next step.

1

List

Farmer lists the harvest with in-app photos and video.

2

Verify

An officer checks the media and grades the crop.

3

Negotiate

Buyer and farmer agree price, quantity and pickup date.

4

Pay advance

Buyer's advance is locked safely in escrow.

5

Dispatch

The nearest suitable vehicle accepts the job.

6

Approve loading

Buyer approves the driver's photos before loading.

7

Track

Live GPS from the farm to the buyer's door.

8

Confirm & settle

Driver and buyer confirm; everyone is paid automatically.
