Project Workflow  
The following detailed workflow outlines the complete transaction lifecycle of HarvestHub. 
It enforces a strict "Payment-First" policy, ensuring the Farmer only begins harvesting after 
the Buyer has financially committed.  
  
Phase 1: Market Analysis & Trusted Listing Creation  
• Step 1.1: Market Insight Analysis  
o Action: The Farmer accesses the Market Insights Dashboard to analyze 
current demand trends (e.g., "High demand for Big Onions in Dambulla").  
o System Status: N/A (Informational View)  
• Step 1.2: Location-Locked Initiation  
o Action: The Farmer initiates a Supply Request. The system performs a 
background GPS Location Check.  
o  Constraint: The Farmer must be physically present at their registered farm 
address to proceed.  
o System Status: Drafting  
Step 1.3: Smart Guided Capture 
• Action: The system activates the Real-Time Camera (Gallery upload is 
disabled). The app displays on-screen guides prompting the Farmer to capture: 
▪ Image 1 (Wide Angle): To show the cultivated field area and crop 
density for estimating the overall harvest volume. 
▪ Image 2 (Close Up): To show the texture, size, and expected quality 
grade of a freshly harvested sample from the field. 
▪ Video Clip (10s): To demonstrate the overall field condition 
alongside a 360-degree view of the harvested sample to prove 
authenticity and freshness. 
• Note: All media is automatically geotagged with GPS coordinates. 
• System Status: Drafting 
 
--- PAGE 2 ---

 
*Step 1.4: Submission 
• Action: The Farmer enters the expected price per kg and the Estimated Total Yield 
(in KG) based on their field size and historical data, then submits the listing. 
• Constraint: The system explicitly labels this as an "Estimated Quantity" on the 
buyer's feed, allowing for a standard real-world variance (e.g., +/- 5%) when the final 
physical weighing occurs at the loading phase. 
• System Status: Pending Verification 
 
 
Step 1.5: Collaborative Officer Quality Check & Queueing 
• Action (Queue Entry): The submitted listing enters a global "Pending 
Verification Queue" that is visible to all registered Agricultural Officers 
on their JavaFX dashboard. 
• Action (Claiming): An available Agricultural Officer selects a listing 
from the queue. The system immediately temporarily locks this listing 
(Status: Under Review) so that no other officer can grade it at the same 
time. 
• Action (Grading): The officer reviews the GPS data and the Smart 
Guided photos. They have three options: 
1. Approve & Grade: Assign a quality grade (e.g., Grade A, B, C). 
2. Request Retake: Ask the farmer to upload a clearer photo 
(Status returns to Drafting). 
3. Reject: Reject the listing if it violates platform guidelines. 
• System Status: Listed (if approved) / Action Required (if 
retake/rejected). 
 
  
Phase 2: Discovery, Negotiation & Scheduling 
• Step 2.1: Initial Engagement (Bidirectional) 
--- PAGE 3 ---

o Action (Scenario A - Supply Driven): A Buyer browses the 
verified marketplace listings and sends a "Purchase Request" to a 
Farmer. 
o Action (Scenario B - Demand Driven): A Farmer browses the 
Buyer "Wanted Ads" and sends a "Supply Offer" to a Buyer, 
detailing their estimated yield and expected price. 
o System Status: Negotiating 
• Step 2.2: Negotiation & Scheduling 
o Action: Following the initial request or offer, the Farmer and 
Buyer use the in-app chat to finalize the exact quantity, agree on 
a final price per kg, and establish a specific Pickup Date. 
o System Status: Accepted (Order is locked, but not paid). 
  
  
Phase 3: Automated Logistics Dispatch & Financial Commitment 
• Step 3.1: Automated Vehicle Selection & Costing 
o Action: Once the Buyer and Farmer confirm the order, the system's Logistics 
Engine reads the total harvest weight. 
o Action: The system eliminates manual selection by automatically filtering and 
assigning the most appropriate vehicle category based on weight capacity. 
o Action: The system fetches GPS coordinates via the Google Maps API and 
automatically calculates a dynamic transport fee Cost = (Distance × Rate) + 
(Weight × HandlingFee). 
o System Status: Awaiting Payment 
Step 3.2: Secure Advance Payment 
• Action: The system calculates the total bill (Total Transport Fee + Partial Crop Cost) 
and automatically adds a 3% Buyer Convenience Fee. 
 
• Action: The Buyer pays this total Advance Payment through the system’s secure 
gateway into the platform's central holding account. 
 
--- PAGE 4 ---

 
• System Status: Payment Processing / Dispatching 
 
• Step 3.3: Smart Dispatch & Harvest Trigger 
o Trigger: Successful Advance Payment verification. 
o Action (Dispatch Loop): The system automatically sends a transport request 
to the nearest available matched Transporter. 
o Scenario A (Accepted): The Transporter accepts. The system sends a "Vehicle 
Assigned - Start Harvesting" push notification to the Farmer. 
o Scenario B (Declined/No Vehicles): The system intelligently reroutes the 
request to the next available driver, or pauses until a vehicle is online. 
o Constraint: The Farmer must not start harvesting until the specific notification 
is received. 
Phase 4: Execution (Harvest, Proof-of-Loading & Transit) 
• Step 4.1: Harvest Completion & Notification Action: The Farmer receives the "Start 
Harvesting" notification, harvests the crops, and packs them. 
o Action: The Farmer taps "Harvest Ready" on the app. 
o Trigger: The system pings the assigned Transporter with the exact GPS pickup 
coordinates. 
o System Status: Ready for Pickup 
Step 4.2: Pre-Loading Quality Verification & Liability Checkpoint 
• Action (Mandatory Evidence): Upon arrival at the farm, the transport Helper 
physically inspects the harvest and captures time-stamped, geotagged photographs of 
the crops before loading them into the vehicle. The Transporter App locks the "Start 
Journey" button until these photos are uploaded. 
 
• Scenario A (Approved): The Buyer remotely reviews and approves the photos via 
their app. The Helper proceeds to load the goods into the vehicle, and the Transporter 
is allowed to press "Start Journey". 
 
--- PAGE 5 ---

• Scenario B (Pre-Loading Rejection): If the Buyer spots poor quality or incorrect 
crops in the photos, they hit "Reject." The loading process is aborted. The Buyer 
receives a 100% refund of their amount, and the Farmer's wallet is automatically 
penalized with an "Empty Trip Fee" to compensate the transport team for the wasted 
trip. 
 
• System Status: Verified & Loaded (If Approved) 
  
--- PAGE 6 ---

Step 4.3: Automated Payment Release & Live Tracking 
• Trigger: The Transporter updates the app status to "Start Journey". 
• Action (Payment Release): The system automatically transfers the Advance Payment 
(Crop Portion) directly into the Farmer's wallet. This guarantees the Farmer gets paid 
the exact second the goods leave their land. 
• Action (Live Tracking): The Transporter app begins sending continuous GPS location 
updates to the Buyer's screen so they can track the delivery in real-time. 
• System Status: In Transit (Live Tracking Active) 
 
 
Phase 5: Delivery, Rejection & Automated Final Settlement 
• Step 5.1: Physical Arrival & Quality Check 
o Action: The vehicle arrives. The Buyer physically inspects the goods against 
the pre-loading photos. 
o Scenario A (Accepted): The Buyer approves the quality and pays the 
remaining 80% balance through the system. 
o Scenario B (Post-Delivery Rejection): The Buyer discovers transit damage 
and hits "Reject Delivery" (Triggering Step 5.3). 
• Step 5.2: Unloading & 2-Way Final Confirmation (If Accepted) 
 
 Action: The Helper unloads the goods. 
 Action (Verification): To prevent delivery fraud, the system requires a digital 
confirmation from both parties present: 
• Driver: Taps "Delivery Completed" on their app at the drop-off location. 
• Buyer: Taps "Goods Received" on their app. 
 Action (Automated Settlement): Once the system receives both confirmations, it acts 
as the central accountant: 
1. Platform Revenue: The system retains the 3% Buyer Convenience Fee. 
2. Farmer Payment: The system transfers the final crop payment to the Farmer (with a 
0% platform fee). 
--- PAGE 7 ---

3. Transporter Payment: The system automatically deducts a 5% Platform 
Commission from the delivery fee and transfers the remaining 95% to the Driver's 
account. 
 Action (Automated Receipt): The system sends an automated notification to the 
Farmer: "Delivery Successful - Funds Transferred." The Farmer does not need to manually 
confirm anything. 
 System Status: Completed / Ready for Rating 
 
 
Step 5.3: Dispute & Return Process 
 
 Action (If Rejected BEFORE Loading - from Step 4.2): Because the crops were poor 
quality before the journey started, the fault lies completely with the Farmer. 
▪ Buyer: Receives a full 100% refund of their advance payment directly from the 
system, including a full refund of the 3% Convenience Fee. 
▪ Transporter: Receives an "Empty Trip Fee" to cover their wasted travel time and 
fuel. 
▪ Farmer (The Penalty): The "Empty Trip Fee" is automatically deducted from the 
Farmer's account. If the Farmer's account does not have enough money, it goes into a 
negative balance and is temporarily suspended. They cannot list new crops until this 
debt is paid back to the system. 
 Action (If Rejected AFTER Delivery - from Step 5.1): Because the crops matched the 
photos at loading but arrived damaged, the fault lies completely with the Transporter. 
▪Buyer: Receives a full 100% refund. The delivery fee and the 3% Convenience Fee 
they paid are fully refunded by the system. The money they paid for the crops is 
automatically deducted from the Transporter's account and given back to the Buyer. 
▪ Farmer: Keeps the original advance payment they received for the crops as 
compensation for their ruined harvest. 
▪ Transporter (The Penalty): Takes full responsibility for the damaged goods. They 
do not get paid for the delivery, and their account is charged to refund the Buyer for 
the ruined crops. If the Transporter's account does not have enough money, it goes into 
--- PAGE 8 ---

a negative balance and is temporarily locked until they pay the system back. They 
earn zero money for the trip. 
 System Status: Return Journey / Disputed 
 
 
Step 5.4: Order Completion & Rating 
• Action (Manual User Rating): Upon completion, the Buyer, Farmer, and Transporter 
can manually rate each other (1 to 5 Stars) based on their personal experience, such as 
communication, punctuality, and crop quality. 
•  Action (Automated System Rating): In the background, the system automatically 
adjusts each user's overall Trust Score based on the transaction data. Users gain 
positive score points for successful, dispute-free deliveries and lose points for canceled 
orders, delayed pickups, or causing refunds. 
• System Status: Closed
